Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, and a display name if you choose to set one.
  • Shift data: food sales, drink sales, charge tips, POS-calculated amounts, and any notes you enter for your own shifts.
  • Restaurant info: the restaurant name and tipout percentages you set up, if you add a restaurant.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or anything from your device beyond what you type into the app.

How we use it

Solely to run the app: calculating your tipout, storing your shift history, and showing you your own stats. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.

Who else touches your data

  • Supabase (our database and login provider) stores your account and shift data securely.
  • Resend (our email provider) sends you sign-in codes. They see your email address only for that purpose.

Neither is authorized to use your data for anything beyond providing these services to us.

Your data, your control

  • You can edit or delete any individual shift at any time.
  • You can delete your entire account and all associated data from inside the app (Menu → Account → Delete Account). This is permanent, immediate, and cannot be undone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at support@dailytipout.app.
